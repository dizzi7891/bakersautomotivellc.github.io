FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Questions from fleet owners

Is the $49 program active today?

It's a proposed pilot offering. Contact Baker's Automotive to confirm eligibility, current pricing, and a written service agreement before relying on the program.

Are repairs included?

No. The proposed membership covers scheduling, tracking, reminders and agreed visual inspections. Diagnostic work, parts and repairs would be priced and approved separately.

Can smaller fleets apply?

Yes. Reach out even if you have fewer than five vehicles; we can discuss regular service arrangements separately.

Will this work for construction and landscape trucks?

That's the intended audience, along with service vans and local delivery vehicles. We will confirm the scope based on the vehicles and work you operate.
