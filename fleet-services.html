Good questions deserve straight answers

Frequently asked.

Do you service small fleets?

Yes, businesses operating a small number of work vehicles are welcome to contact the shop about service needs. Proposed membership eligibility and minimums remain subject to confirmation.

Are repairs included in the monthly price?

No. The proposed pilot is focused on organization, inspection and maintenance reminders. Repairs, diagnostic work, parts and labor would be estimated separately.

Can I bring a vehicle in without joining?

Yes. FleetCare inquiries and normal commercial repair inquiries are welcome. Contact the shop to discuss your vehicle and schedule.

Can I approve estimates online?

We're developing a private shop-management and fleet portal. Online approvals and secure accounts are planned, but are not yet offered as a live customer service.
